FAQ

Quick answers before you reach out.

What do you need from me to start?

Your raw footage, a short description of what you want, and any reference videos you like. Music and brand assets help too.

How long does an edit take?

It depends on length and complexity. Short-form edits are usually quick, and we can agree on a deadline before I start.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. Send your feedback after the first cut and I'll revise it until you're happy.

Which platforms do you edit for?

Instagram Reels, YouTube and Shorts, TikTok, and long-form podcasts and gaming content.
